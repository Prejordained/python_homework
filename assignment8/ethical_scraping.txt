1. Wikipedia restricts a lot of internal and administrative pages.
The main ones I noticed are special pages (/wiki/Special:),
backend wiki pages (/w/), API endpoints (/api/), deletion
discussion pages, arbitration pages, and various admin
noticeboard pages. Basically anything that is internal
Wikipedia business rather than actual article content.

2. Yes. Lots of specific bots are completely blocked, including
wget, HTTrack, and several others that were flagged for
making too many requests or behaving badly. SemrushBot is
allowed but has to wait 5 seconds between requests. A couple
of Wikipedia's own work bots have no restrictions at all.

3. The robots.txt file is basically a website's way of telling
scrapers what they are and are not allowed to access. It
protects the server from being slammed with too many requests
and keeps private pages from being scraped. As a developer
you should always check robots.txt before scraping a site
because ignoring it can get your IP blocked and could even
cause legal problems.
